# 09_Portfolio_Stress_Reporting.sas

## Overview

This program generates portfolio-level stress testing reports using the Expected Credit Loss (ECL) results produced by the Banking Stress Testing Framework.

The objective is to aggregate, summarize, and analyze stressed portfolio results across scenarios, products, and risk segments.

This program transforms detailed risk calculations into management-ready reporting outputs.

---

## Purpose

The program produces summarized portfolio views that support:

- Executive Reporting
- Credit Risk Monitoring
- Stress Testing Analysis
- Capital Planning
- Portfolio Review
- Regulatory Reporting

The reporting outputs serve as the primary source for Power BI dashboard visualizations.

---

## Business Importance

Stress testing calculations become meaningful only when converted into actionable business insights.

Portfolio reporting enables stakeholders to:

- Measure overall portfolio risk
- Compare scenarios
- Identify high-risk products
- Analyze risk concentration
- Understand loss drivers
- Support decision-making

---

## Inputs

### ECL Dataset

Generated from:

```text
08_ECL_Calculation.sas
```

Containing:

- Exposure at Default (EAD)
- Stressed PD
- Stressed LGD
- Expected Credit Loss (ECL)
- Scenario Information
- Product Information
- Risk Segment Information

---

## Processing Activities

### Portfolio-Level Aggregation

Calculates:

```text
Total Exposure

Total Base ECL

Total Stressed ECL

Incremental Stress Loss
```

for each scenario.

---

### Scenario Reporting

Generates portfolio summaries for:

```text
Baseline

Mild_Stress

Severe_Stress
```

allowing direct comparison of stress impacts.

---

### Product-Level Analysis

Aggregates ECL results for products including:

```text
Mortgage

Personal Loan

Auto Loan

Credit Card

SME Loan

Corporate Loan
```

to identify key loss contributors.

---

### Risk Segment Analysis

Groups portfolio losses by:

```text
Low Risk

Medium Risk

High Risk
```

to measure risk concentration.

---

### Comparative Analysis

Calculates:

- Base vs Stressed ECL
- Incremental Stress Loss
- Product-Level Loss Changes
- Segment-Level Loss Changes
