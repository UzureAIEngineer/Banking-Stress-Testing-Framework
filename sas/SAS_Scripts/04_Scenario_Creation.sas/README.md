
# 04_Scenario_Creation.sas

## Overview

This program creates macroeconomic stress scenarios for the Banking Stress Testing Framework.

The objective is to define multiple economic environments and establish the assumptions that will be applied during portfolio stress testing.

This program serves as the foundation for downstream Probability of Default (PD), Loss Given Default (LGD), and Expected Credit Loss (ECL) stress calculations.

---

## Purpose

The program creates standardized economic scenarios used to evaluate portfolio resilience under changing economic conditions.

The scenarios provide a structured mechanism to simulate:

- Economic downturns
- Rising unemployment
- Increasing inflation
- Interest rate shocks
- Deteriorating borrower credit quality

---

## Business Importance

Stress testing helps financial institutions understand how adverse economic conditions may impact portfolio performance.

The scenario framework supports:

- Risk Management
- Regulatory Reporting
- Capital Planning
- Portfolio Monitoring
- Early Warning Risk Assessment

---

## Scenarios Created

### Baseline Scenario

Represents normal economic conditions.

Characteristics:

- Stable economic growth
- Normal unemployment levels
- Stable borrower performance
- Standard risk environment

---

### Mild Stress Scenario

Represents moderate economic deterioration.

Characteristics:

- Reduced GDP growth
- Increased inflation
- Mild rise in unemployment
- Moderate increase in portfolio risk

---

### Severe Stress Scenario

Represents significant economic disruption.

Characteristics:

- Economic contraction
- Sharp unemployment increases
- Reduced consumer spending
- Elevated portfolio credit risk
- Significant deterioration in borrower quality

---
