Business Problem Statement

Project Title

Banking Stress Testing Framework

Background

Financial institutions operate in dynamic economic environments where adverse macroeconomic conditions can significantly impact the credit quality of loan portfolios.

Events such as:

Economic recession
Rising unemployment
Inflation spikes
Interest rate increases
Housing market downturns
can lead to increased customer defaults and substantial financial losses.

To proactively manage these risks, banks perform periodic stress testing exercises to evaluate portfolio resilience under unfavorable economic scenarios.

Business Challenge
The bank currently maintains a diversified portfolio consisting of:

Mortgage Loans
Personal Loans
Auto Loans
Credit Cards
SME Loans
Management requires a structured framework to assess:

Impact of changing economic conditions
Changes in borrower default behavior
Impact on expected portfolio losses
Capital adequacy under stress scenarios
Concentration risk across products and customer segments
Without a formal stress testing framework, the institution may underestimate future portfolio risk and face unexpected capital pressures.

Business Objectives
The organization aims to:

Risk Identification
Identify customer segments most vulnerable to economic deterioration.

Loss Estimation
Estimate potential portfolio losses under various stress scenarios.

Capital Planning
Assess whether current capital reserves are sufficient to absorb losses.

Regulatory Readiness
Support internal and external regulatory risk reporting requirements.

Strategic Decision Making
Enable management to implement risk mitigation actions before portfolio deterioration occurs.

Scope
Included
Loan portfolio analysis
Customer segmentation
Macroeconomic scenario analysis
PD estimation
LGD estimation
Expected Loss calculation
Portfolio impact assessment
Excluded
Real-time risk monitoring
Market risk modeling
Liquidity stress testing
Fraud analytics
Key Risk Metrics
The framework evaluates:

Probability of Default (PD)
Loss Given Default (LGD)
Exposure at Default (EAD)
Expected Loss (EL)
Delinquency Rate
Non-Performing Asset Ratio
Capital Impact
Expected Outcome
Upon completion, the framework will provide:

Portfolio-level risk assessment
Segment-level risk analysis
Scenario comparison reports
Stressed expected loss estimates
Management recommendations
Regulatory support reporting
Business Benefits
Improved risk visibility
Better capital planning
Early warning identification
Enhanced regulatory compliance
More informed strategic decision-making

Conclusion
Stress testing enables financial institutions to proactively assess the resilience of their portfolios and prepare for adverse economic conditions. This framework serves as a structured analytical solution to support risk management and business decision-making.
